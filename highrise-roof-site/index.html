    05 / Questions

    Before you call.

    My roof is leaking right now. What do I do?

    Call 347-731-6076. Our emergency line is open 24/7, including nights and weekends.

    Is the estimate really free?

    Yes. We come out, look at the roof and give you a detailed written estimate at no charge.

    Which areas do you serve?

    Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island.

    Can you help with an insurance claim after storm damage?

    Yes. We assess storm damage and help homeowners with the insurance claim.

    Do you offer financing?

    Financing options are available. Ask about them when you request your estimate.

    Is your work under warranty?

    All of our work comes with a warranty. The terms depend on the system installed, and we spell them out in your estimate.

    What does GAF Gold Elite mean for me?

    GAF is one of the largest roofing manufacturers in North America. Its Gold Elite Commercial certification goes to contractors who meet its standards for licensing, insurance and installation quality.
